About FarmHisab

Your digital farm record and management companion

FarmHisab is a farm management application designed to help farmers maintain their farm records in an organized and convenient way.

The app helps manage fields, crops, farm expenses, income, labour, cotton picking, inventory, spray and fertilizer records, tasks, reports and historical farm data.

FarmHisab is designed with an offline-first approach, allowing important farm records to remain available without requiring a continuous internet connection.

FarmHisab Features

Everything you need to manage your farm records

🌾

Field & Crop Management

Manage fields, crops, sowing dates, harvests and crop history year-wise.

💰

Farm Expenses

Record spraying, fertilizer, labour, machinery and other farm expenses.

📦

Inventory

Manage pesticides, fertilizers, seeds, fungicides, herbicides, tonics and other farm stock.

💊

Spray Records

Record medicines, quantities, pumps, costs and spray activity history.

🧪

Fertilizer Records

Maintain fertilizer usage, quantity, cost and crop-wise application history.

👷

Labour Management

Manage labour attendance, work, wages and payment records.

🌿

Cotton Picking

Record labour-wise cotton picking quantity, rate per kg and individual payments.

📊

Crop Analytics

View crop-wise expenses, labour costs, picking costs, income and profit.

📅

Year History

Select a year and view historical crop and activity records date-wise.

💵

Income Tracking

Record farm sales, quantities, rates and crop-wise income.

🔔

Tasks & Reminders

Create farm tasks, mark them completed and receive reminders.

💾

Backup & Restore

Backup important farm records and restore them when required.
