Farm Hisab – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Farm Hisab, developed by Shekhar Kuttarmare, handles information when you use the Farm Hisab mobile application.

1. Information stored in the app

Farm Hisab is designed as an offline-first farm management application. Information you enter, such as fields, crops, farm expenses, income, labour records, cotton picking records, inventory, spray/fertilizer records, tasks, crop diary information and related photos, is primarily stored locally on your device.

2. Photos and media

If you choose to add photos to crop, spray or fertilizer records, the app uses the Android system media/file picker. Photos are used to display the records you create. Farm Hisab does not require access to your entire photo library.

3. Backup and restore

The app provides backup and restore functionality. Backup files are created so you can preserve and restore farm records. You control where you save or share backup files.

4. Notifications

Farm Hisab may schedule local task reminders so that you can receive task notifications even when the app is not open. These reminders are generated on your device.

5. Advertising

Farm Hisab may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device/app information and ad interaction information as described in Google's applicable policies. Where required, the app provides privacy/consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

6. Internet access

The core farm-record features are designed to work offline. Internet access may be used for advertising and other online services. If internet access is unavailable, ads or online services may not function, while offline farm-record features can continue to work.

7. Data sharing

Farm Hisab does not sell your farm records. Information may be processed by third-party services used by the app, such as Google AdMob, where necessary to provide advertising and related services.

8. Data security and retention

Farm records stored locally remain on your device unless you choose to export, back up or share them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove locally stored records. Keep a backup if you need to retain them.

9. Children's privacy

Farm Hisab is a general farm-management application and is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

10. Your choices

You can choose whether to enter information, create or share backups, add photos, and interact with advertisements. Where available, you can manage advertising privacy/consent choices from the app.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, services or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
