Privacy Policy – FarmHisab

Effective date: 17 November 2026

FarmHisab is a farm management application that helps users maintain agricultural records, including crops, fields, inventory, labour, attendance, income, expenses, cotton picking, notes, tasks and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, you may enter farm and business information such as field and crop details, farming expenses and income, inventory and stock records, labour and attendance records, cotton picking and payment records, notes, tasks and reminders.

FarmHisab is designed to store these farm records locally on your device. Do not enter information you are not authorised to store.

2. Photos and Media

The current version of FarmHisab does not provide a feature to attach photos or media to farm records. The app does not need photo-library access for this functionality.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

FarmHisab uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising services may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions and diagnostic information to provide, measure and protect advertising services, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about advertising.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent messages and privacy choices. The choices available may depend on your location and applicable law.

4. Internet Access and Offline Use

Core farm record features are designed to work offline. An internet connection may be used for advertising and related services, and for any other online feature that is clearly presented in the app.

5. Backup and Restore

If you use the backup feature, the app may create a backup file containing farm records. You choose where to save or share that file. Anyone who obtains the backup file may be able to access its contents, so keep it secure. Storage providers and apps you choose to share the file with are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Notifications

If you enable reminders, FarmHisab may use Android notification functionality to show task or reminder notifications. You can manage notification permission through your device settings.

7. How Information Is Shared

FarmHisab does not sell your farm records. Farm records are intended to remain on your device unless you choose to export or share a backup, or a specific feature clearly explains otherwise. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google Mobile Ads as described above.

8. Data Security

Farm records are stored on your device. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep exported backup files in a secure location. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Managing and Deleting Your Data

You can manage or delete records using the available features in the app. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it may remove locally stored records. Backup files exported from the app must be deleted separately from the location where you saved them.

10. Children's Privacy

FarmHisab is a general farm-management and productivity app and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not intentionally request children to provide personal information to a FarmHisab-operated account system. Advertising services may apply their own age-related policies and restrictions.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if app features, service providers or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
